# Pandit verification runbook

Only authorised administrators may review private onboarding evidence. Never copy full identity or bank numbers into notes.

1. Compare the legal name, date of birth and photograph with the submitted government ID. Record the verification method and only a masked reference.
2. Open every required private document through its five-minute signed link. Reject unreadable, expired or inconsistent evidence with a specific correction note.
3. Contact at least one submitted temple/customer reference using independently confirmed contact details. Record the outcome; do not mark the reference verified merely because a letter was uploaded.
4. Complete the Puja knowledge check consistently using the same internal rubric and record a 0–100 score.
5. Verify UPI ownership or bank account name using an approved provider or a documented manual method. Record a masked provider/reference result.
6. Resolve all mismatches and ensure every checklist item is `VERIFIED` before approval. Approval, rejection, changes and checklist edits are audit logged.
7. Re-review the Pandit after a payout-detail change, serious complaint, identity mismatch, or long inactivity.

External government-ID and bank verification require a contracted compliant provider. Until that exists, keep the pilot manual, limited and consistently documented.
